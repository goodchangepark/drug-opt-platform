# Prediction Engine v1

`drugopt-prediction-engine-v1@1.0.0` is the frozen global, pre-experimental prediction policy. Its machine-readable source is `validation/stage4e4_prediction_engine_v1_freeze.json`; its SHA-256 is generated from canonical JSON, not a timestamp. The executable endpoint plan remains `PredictionOrchestrator → endpoint strategy registry → CORE → authorized SHADOW → production selection → AD/confidence → immutable prospective freeze`.

The complete 49-endpoint strategy, model, version, unit, evidence class, validation state, reliability, AD policy, calibration state, limitations, rollback target, and freeze status are in `stage4e4_engine_v1_endpoint_policy_matrix.json`. This avoids a misleading compressed table and is the authoritative inventory.

Core decisions are intentionally conservative: solubility is M1 single-core (ESOL shadow; RDKIT-GBR excluded); Caco-2 is `admetica_caco2` single-core and closed; PPB is its current single-core model; HLM/RLM/MLM remain species-isolated OpenADMET single-core outputs; CYP endpoints are independently scoped; CYP3A4's 0.9578/0.0422 blend is research-only; P-gp inhibitor remains single-core; hERG is raw M1 at threshold 0.50, with Platt and M2 only research/calibration support. Ames and DILI retain their qualified single-core probability models.

Metabolic soft spots use SyGMa plus SMARTCyp `RANK_FUSION`, never a raw-score average. Metabolite hypotheses are SyGMa `RULE_BASED`. pKa is `IonizationEngine_v1` `RULE_ESTIMATE`; logD7.4 is `DERIVED_ESTIMATE` from cLogP, pKa, and simplified ionization. RDKit properties are deterministic calculations, not ML predictions. NCA, IVIVE, Vd/absorption, simulation, and human translation remain mechanistic/translational streams and are never converted into generic ADMET consensus.

Reliability is multidimensional. The reliability matrix preserves numeric accuracy, ranking ability, calibration, AD, validation strength, evidence class, and limitations separately. Caco-2 has limited ranking ability (ExpansionRx MAE 0.5695, RMSE 0.7457, Spearman 0.0410). hERG has limited discrimination/calibration (raw M1 remains production). HLM/RLM external evidence is limited and MLM evidence is insufficient. pKa/logD are not validated quantitative ML. MODEL_UNAVAILABLE is a first-class result: no transporter, cross-species, or neighboring-endpoint substitution is permitted.

Shadows are evidence collection only and cannot affect the production value. Their role and rationale are in `stage4e4_engine_v1_model_roles.json`; active runtime status alone never makes a model production eligible. The unavailable endpoint list is separately frozen in `stage4e4_engine_v1_unavailable_endpoints.json`.

Each new prediction freeze records compound-version identity, structure hash, standardizer, policy ID/hash, endpoint strategy, core model/version, authorized shadows, production value, AD/confidence metadata, timestamp, and record identity. Freeze IDs are policy-version namespaced, so new Engine-v1 executions cannot overwrite Stage 4D historical freezes. Experimental feedback is stored separately and cannot rewrite a frozen prediction. Any future adaptation must observe the hierarchy GLOBAL → PROJECT → SERIES → LOCAL and use prior compounds only.

Rollback is exact: commit `91fbf21`, policy `stage4d4-endpoint-strategy-v1`. Engine v1 is ready for controlled internal prospective validation only. It is not clinically validated, regulatory qualified, universally accurate, guaranteed OOD chemistry, a replacement for experimental ADMET, or capable of predicting unavailable transporters.
