The live Help page is authoritative for installed versions and availability. It is generated by `/api/help/registry` from the runtime package inventory, Model Registry, PK Method Registry, and Capability Registry. This document is a stabilization snapshot, not a second registry.

## Current workflow

Drug-OPT supports project-isolated compound registration and CompoundVersion history; standardized structures and calculated properties; experimental activity and SAR; installed ADMET, metabolism, transporter, and safety predictions; medicinal chemistry strategy and analog generation; experimental PK and NCA; IVIVE; IV, PO, SC, and IP simulation; cross-species scaling; human translational PK; prospective freezing; and retrospective validation.

Predictions and calculations are decision-support evidence. They do not replace experimental studies.

## Major limitations

- No full PBPK, PK/PD, or animal efficacy prediction.
- No qualified quantitative ML pKa model; the current estimate is rule-based.
- Several transporter and optional safety endpoints have no qualified local model.
- Conformal calibration is unavailable or undercovered for some operational models.
- Microsomal clearance confidence is LOW.
- Soft spots, metabolite hypotheses, and generated analogs require experimental validation.
